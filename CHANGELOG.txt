Changelog

0.1.3

Illuminate now deactivates its own combo after use. The previous reference
instead disabled Exhilaration and left Illuminate usable again. This repair
removes that repeat-use behavior and preserves the unrelated combo.

Shadow Puppets now gives its existing instant-attack effect to the linked
Dark Paragon participant. The original self-targeting failed its required
character tag on Spectre. Other allies are not granted the participant
effect, and Spectre retains its existing effect. The effect now expires
when Dark Paragon attacks, preventing repeated instant attacks until hit.

Hard Reset English:
Before: Wipe all enemy buffs, wipe all hero debuffs, recharge all hero shields.
After: Wipe all enemy buffs and all hero debuffs.
The cleanse already works. The referenced recharge effect is missing, and
its intended strength is unknown. This update corrects the description;
it does not introduce a guessed shield gain.

The remaining enemy references were investigated with native methods and
current tables. Bounty Hunter trimming introduces a self-retry loop; missing
reload powers, a MOTHER continuation and Commodore's Delicate effect require
more evidence before replacement. No additional enemy mechanics are changed.

The repository README now covers both downloads and their log paths.

0.1.2

Weighted enemy action lists now retain their weights. The original method
removed numbers before interpreting the list, leaving equal chances for
the surviving actions. The correction uses the game RNG and preserves a
no-choice gap when declared weights total less than 100. Unweighted lists
retain their original behavior.

Dragoon Overseer's summon chain now references the existing continuation
action instead of a power ID. Panzer's repair chain now references the
existing area attack action instead of a missing action. These repairs
can make encounters harder.

The installer supports separate Bugfix and Rebalance releases. The
Rebalance build compiles the same correction source. It includes all
forecast, achievement, item and enemy fixes from the Bugfix build.

Loading failures, uncertain enemy references and broader forecast issues
remain under investigation.

0.1.1

Corrects two additional combat forecast discrepancies:

- Effects such as Marked applied by an earlier planned attack were absent
  from later vulnerability checks. The forecast now tracks projected
  target effects and relevant CRIT-defense changes without changing the
  live unit. Effect expiry and removal affecting other characters remain
  outside these corrections.
- Reflection estimates omitted the incoming attack's CRIT bonus. Both
  the reaction display and the full-turn forecast now include it.

Retries earned counter achievements after Steam becomes ready or a
submission fails. Existing local counters determine eligibility; this
does not add progress or unlock unearned achievements.

Retries are limited to three attempts per earned achievement in each
Steam-ready session, at least ten seconds apart. New readiness sessions
permit another attempt. Ordered delayed store callbacks retain their
submission owner across profile changes and component reactivation.

Repository and release documentation now uses plain-text files.
Loading, controller and cutscene softlocks remain outside this update.

0.1.0

First test release. Includes the item-reference, Paragon shield-steal, Aegis text,
relationship-preview, missing-affix-pool and blaster-tag repairs listed in the README.

Corrects three combat forecast defects:

- Reflected damage could reduce the attacker's armor in the forecast even
though execution leaves it intact. Later attacks could consequently show too
much health damage.

- Direct attack inspection passed damage as a positive resource change to the HUD,
making damage look like healing. Its armor delta also used maximum armor instead of
the estimated remaining armor.

- Resisted delay rounded fractions to the nearest second in the forecast while execution
discarded the fraction. Two 15-second staggers against 50% resistance could forecast a
45-second attack at 61 seconds when it actually executes at 59.

The corrections change estimates and display inputs. They do not change executed damage, delay or round boundaries.
