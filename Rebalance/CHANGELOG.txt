Changelog

0.3.1

First public installer package. No earlier version is required.
Includes Bugfix Mod 0.1.2's item, localization, camping-preview, combat
forecast, achievement and enemy repairs from the same source files.

Replaces 288 equipment pool-reference workarounds with the shared repair
of 36 missing pools. These use matching sword pool definitions with the
weapon slot changed. The 336 damage-trait corrections and Aegis English
correction also move to the shared patch. Saved random rolls are retained.

Retains all approved character and variant changes from the private 0.3
build, including Mind Scythe damage 10 per hit and the higher-level kit
adjustments. Retains the following combo and camping changes:

Freebooters: Fury 35 to 25.
Surge and Protect: Fury 25 to 20; shields 15 to 25 per valid ally, capped.
Oppressive Fire: damage 25 to 30 per hit, three hits.
Branded For Life: damage 10 to 15 per hit, four hits.
Bulletstorm: damage 25 to 30 per hit, three hits.
Truth and Justice: damage 20 to 25 per hit, four hits.
Law of the Jungle: damage 60 to 75, one hit.
Demonopolize: Fury 20 to 15.

Damage values are ability-record bases before level and other modifiers.
Hit counts, action consumption, self-deactivation and effect requirements
are unchanged. Multiple hits do not add a flurry tag.

Pamphlet: camping cost 2 to 1.
Soul Soothing: camping cost 2 to 1.
Optimizations: camping cost 2 to 1.
Reborn: camping cost 3 to 2.

No new character nerfs or unlock changes are included. Exact before/after
values are in balance-changes.json in the source release. The combined
loader applies 480 field changes and 61 added records, and rolls back both
overlays if activation fails.
