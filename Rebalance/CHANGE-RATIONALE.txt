Why these changes

Character changes were approved after reviewing the Steam 1.5.1.5 data,
level scaling, alternate forms and kit competition. The aim is stronger
starter choices on Entropy V while retaining distinct roles. Buffs are the
default; this release adds no new character nerfs. Numerical improvements
are not proof that every form is equally effective in actual runs.

The inherited starter pass improves weak early attack, control or recovery
options and reduces severe starting penalties on selected progeny. The
later pass improves Marksman shield buffers and Killshot timing, allows
Commando Salvo to reach the back row, improves Archon shield timing and
retained defenses, restores Dayana's starting speed benefit and repairs
Durnin's haste/stagger tag matching. Selected Enforcer armor piercing and
starting defense adjustments improve early contribution.

The higher-level kit pass preserves alternatives after starter buffs.
Mind Scythe gains damage 10 per hit because the Psion Knife buff removed
its neutral-target damage advantage on Dinah and Viera at level 10. Its
three-hit total becomes 30 before scaling. Psion Knife retains its speed
and control role. English trait summaries and ability descriptions were
updated where their meaning changed.

Camping

Pamphlet becomes affordable early shield preparation at one point. Soul
Soothing becomes cheaper protection after shields fail; it provides no
immediate healing. Optimizations' half armor repair and shield defense
become easier to fit alongside other preparation. Reborn's full healing
costs two points, allowing it to fit alongside a three-point group card.
The five-point camping budget remains. Cheaper cards can accelerate
relationships by allowing more plays; that remains a playtesting concern.

Combos

Freebooters: cheaper access to confusion setup after spending two actions.
Surge and Protect: a larger immediate shield component and lower Fury cost
make recovery easier to justify. Group size and shield caps still matter.
Oppressive Fire: better payoff for spending an Aegis defensive action and
an Enforcer action, with the same three hits.
Branded For Life: raises small-hit damage that armor heavily reduces;
its permanent Mark still requires CRIT.
Bulletstorm: improves the payoff of instant focus fire over separate attacks.
Truth and Justice: improves its close-range burst while retaining reach limits.
Law of the Jungle: improves ordinary burst while retaining its piercing,
shield damage and stagger requirements.
Demonopolize: makes short-lived damage suppression cheaper without
extending its duration or removing its CRIT requirement.

Items and shared fixes

Broken Focus Cortex and Disruptor Implant references omit their declared
damage bonuses. Repairing the names restores existing tier-appropriate
traits. Missing affix pools are restored from the working sword pool at
the same grade and level, with the weapon slot changed. This follows the
surviving override pattern; exact developer intent is an inference.
Previously saved empty rolls are not filled retroactively.

The shared corrections also repair Paragon shield-steal stacking, Aegis
health-regeneration text, card rank previews, specific forecast defects
and missed earned achievement submissions. Weighted enemy choices and
two broken continuations are corrected even when that increases difficulty.
Uncertain enemy references and loading recovery remain held for further
investigation. The release does not invent missing effects.
